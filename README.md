# GenEd Studio

GenEd Studio is an AI-powered platform for generating personalized educational videos. Users can create their own teaching persona—complete with custom video and voice—and generate engaging, narrative-driven educational content with synchronized transcripts and rich visuals.

## Features

- **Persona Creation:**  
	Users can upload their own video and voice to create a unique teaching persona.

- **AI-Generated Educational Content:**  
	Generate professional-grade educational videos from your transcript or prompt, tailored to your persona.

- **Vector Search Optimization:**  
	The platform now uses vector search to optimize and accelerate the video generation process, ensuring relevant and high-quality visual content.

- **Rich Visual Storytelling:**  
	Videos are generated with synchronized transcripts, AI-generated images, and video segments for a seamless learning experience.

## How It Works

1. **Sign Up & Create Persona:**  
	 Register and upload your video and voice to build your digital teaching persona.

2. **Generate Content:**  
	 Enter your educational transcript or prompt. The system breaks it into meaningful segments and generates corresponding visuals and narration.

3. **Optimized Video Generation:**  
	 With vector search, the platform efficiently matches transcript segments to the most relevant visuals, speeding up content creation.

4. **Download or Share:**  
	 Receive a polished educational video ready for download or sharing.
